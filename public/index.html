How to play

  1. Invite one friend. Create a room and send the 5-character code or link.
  2. Take turns. Red goes first. Tap a column to drop your disc into the lowest open space.
  3. Connect four. Get four of your color horizontally, vertically, or diagonally to win.
  4. Play again. Both players must choose Play again to start a new round.

A full board with no four-in-a-row is a draw. You can only move on your turn.
